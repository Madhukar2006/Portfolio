# Support

If you have a question, find a problem, or want to suggest an improvement:

### 💬 Questions

Open a GitHub Issue with enough information for the problem to be understood.

### 🐛 Bug Reports

Please include:

- What happened
- What you expected
- Steps to reproduce the issue
- Browser/device information when relevant

### 💡 Suggestions

Feature suggestions and improvements are welcome.

Please explain:

- What you would like to improve
- Why it would be useful
- How you think it could work

For general questions, you can also reach out through the contact links available on my portfolio.

Thank you! 🚀
